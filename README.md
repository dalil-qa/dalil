# Dalil: crew health, guided by evidence

Team Dalil's entry for NASA Space Apps 2026 (Doha), challenge: health monitoring software for astronauts on space missions.

**Status:** skeleton only. The app is in `app/`; the Coach Worker goes in `worker/` and the station firmware in `firmware/`.

**Not a medical device: guidance only.**

Sources, AI use and licence are documented here as the project grows. Licence: MIT (see `LICENSE`).
