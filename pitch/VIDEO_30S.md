# The 30-second video for NASA Space Apps

**What it's for:** the project demo on our Space Apps submission. The global judges (and the Doha panel, who score "the submitted project and its demo together") may see **only this video**, so it has to tell the whole story without us in the room.

## Rules (from the Space Apps 2025 Project Submission Guide; check the 2026 form when submitting)
- **30 seconds or less. A demo over the limit is not eligible for global judging.** Aim for **28 s** so nothing gets cut.
- **English subtitles** on screen (burn them in).
- Hosted outside Space Apps (YouTube or Google Drive), with a **public link**. Open it in a private browser window to check.
- Our own safety rules: **no copyrighted music** (use none, or a royalty-free track from the editor's library), **no NASA logo**, label the mission history **"Sample mission data"**, and only film people who agreed to be in it.

---

## The story in one line
*On the way to Mars, the doctor is up to 22 minutes away. Baseera, a 30-second morning check, catches the change days early, cites NASA, and works offline, in space and at home in Qatar.*

## How to record it

### Phone shots (0:00–0:10)
- **Landscape**, 1080p, 30 fps. Phone on a tripod, or wedged against books. No handheld zooms.
- Bright, even light from a window or a desk lamp in front of the actor, not behind.
- Clean background: a desk, the station, nothing else. Wipe the station first.
- Film each shot **3 times** and keep the best. Film 2 seconds before and after each action, so there's room to cut.
- Close-up of the finger: fill the frame with the station so the box's screen is readable. Tap to focus on it.
- The station must be really measuring (plugged into the laptop with a check running), so the shot is genuine.

### Screen recording (0:10–0:28)
- Laptop at **1920×1080**, Chrome full screen (`F11`), zoom 100%, notifications off (Focus assist on).
- Record with **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar), or OBS. Record each screen as a separate clip of about 10 s, moving the mouse slowly.
- Start from **Settings → reset to the Day 47 Mars demo**, then pick **Crew Member 2**. For Home Mode, switch it on in Settings and press **العربية**.
- Hover over or click what the voice-over names, at the moment it's named.

### Voice-over
- Record on a phone held 20 cm from the mouth, in a quiet room with soft furnishings (a car or a room with curtains works well). Abdullah or Sulaiman, whoever sounds calmer.
- Read the whole script 3 times in one take; use the best. Speak slower than feels natural.

### Editing (about 30 minutes)
- **Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11) or **CapCut**. Both can add subtitles automatically; **check every word**, especially "Baseera", "NASA-STD-3001" and the numbers.
- Order: lay down the voice-over first, then place the clips to match it.
- Subtitles: white text with a dark box, bottom centre, large enough to read on a phone.
- Export 1080p MP4, check the length is **≤ 0:29**, then upload.
- YouTube title: *Baseera (بصيرة): crew health, guided by evidence · NASA Space Apps 2026 Doha*. Visibility **Public** (or Unlisted if the form accepts it). Paste the link in the submission form and open it once in a private window.
