### 1. Project Context (Keep it simple, non-technical):
- **Team & Project Name:** **Team Baseera (بصيرة)** (*insight / foresight*, seeing clearly, in Arabic); project **Dalil** (*guide* and *evidence* in Arabic). It is a 30-second morning health check station (a small biometric sensor box measuring heart rate, blood oxygen, and skin temperature) connected to a simple web app.
- **How it works (Simple):** Instead of relying on generic medical averages, Dalil learns each user's *personal baseline*, flags subtle anomalies early, provides NASA-backed health guidance offline, and simulates real communication delays (like the up-to-22-minute Mars one-way delay) to give immediate first-step actions while waiting for a doctor.
- **Dual Impact (Space + Qatar):** Built to keep astronauts safe on deep-space missions, and directly adaptable as a simple home health check (with built-in **Arabic RTL & English Home Mode**) for elderly people in Qatar.
- **Research & Statistics to Include:**
  - **Up to 22 minutes** one-way communication delay between Mars and Earth (about 3 minutes at the closest; up to **44 minutes** round-trip; 14 minutes one-way / 28 minutes round-trip used in the app's Mars transit example).
  - According to the **WHO**, Qatar’s population aged 60+ will grow more than **tenfold** (from ~60,000 to nearly 690,000 by 2050).
  - According to the **International Diabetes Federation (IDF)**, **24.6%** (nearly 1 in 4) of adults in Qatar have diabetes, and **32.9%** of Qatari nationals have high blood pressure (STEPwise Survey).

### 2. Strict Pitch & Slide Design Rules (MUST FOLLOW):
- **Duration:** About **2 minutes 50 seconds**, inside the **3-minute** slot (around 350 words spoken at a calm, confident pace).
- **Language & Tone:** Simple, clear, human, and accessible English (B2 level). **Avoid heavy technical jargon** (do not talk about baud rates, I2C, or complex code). Focus on human impact, numbers, and execution.
- **3-Word Memory Anchor:** Repeat 3 core keywords throughout the pitch so judges remember them: **"Personal. Sourced. Immediate."**
- **Opening Hook:** Never start with our names. Start with a powerful number/storytelling hook (e.g., the 44-minute silence in space) to grab immediate attention.
- **Strategic Pauses:** Mark **[PAUSE - 3 SECONDS]** in the script **at most twice**—only right before or after dropping a major "bomb" statistic or realization.
- **Slide 0 (Silent Title Slide):** Design a silent opening slide shown before we speak. It displays only the **Dalil Logo**, a crisp tagline (e.g., *"Evidence-Based Health Guidance, From Deep Space to Every Home"*), and badges showing: **Category: HealthTech | Stage: Working MVP**.
- **Visual & Formatting Rules for All Slides:**
  - **NO Dark Mode** (use a clean, bright, high-contrast light background with large, readable fonts).
  - **NO Videos** in the presentation.
  - Indicate exact spots to place **high-empathy photos** (e.g., an isolated astronaut, an elderly person in Qatar) instead of walls of text.
  - Include a **Mind Map slide** visually connecting Dalil's core flow (30-Sec Check → Personal Baseline → NASA-Backed Guidance → Space & Qatar Impact).
  - Include a **Competitive Analysis Table** comparing Dalil against existing solutions (e.g., Standard Smartwatches, Telehealth Apps, Traditional Clinic Visits). Use checkmarks (✔) and crosses (✘). **Crucial rule:** Make the **very last row** our ultimate differentiator—a feature that **ONLY Dalil has (✔)** and all competitors lack (✘), such as *"Offline Personal-Baseline Triage + Signal-Delay Guidance"*.
- **Stage & Delivery Cues:** Embed brief cues inside the script for vocal tone shifts, slow confident pacing, hand gestures, and where to point on the slides. Also include a 1-sentence note on why our team can execute even if conditions change (selling the team's resilience, not just the idea).
